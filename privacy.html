Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 September 2026Last updated: 4 September 2026

Colourway is an iOS app that turns receipts into a cost-basis ledger for resale inventory. This policy describes what it collects, where that goes, and how to get rid of it. It describes the app as it actually behaves; where a feature does not exist yet, this policy does not describe one.

Colourway is operated by Break Thruu Entertainment, Inc., Ontario, Canada. Contact: privacy@colourwayapp.com.

The short version

  • You give Colourway an email address and photographs of your receipts.
  • Receipt images are sent to Anthropic's API to be read, then stored in your account.
  • Everything else is derived from those receipts, or is a setting you chose.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of it, immediately, from inside the app.

Information read from your receipts

Receipt images frequently contain personal information, and Colourway extracts and stores parts of it so your ledger is usable:

  • Merchant name and country
  • Purchase date and order number
  • Currency, subtotal, tax, shipping, duties, discount, and total
  • The last four digits of the payment card, where the receipt prints them
  • Payment method (for example "Visa debit")
  • Line items: description, quantity, unit price, brand, style code, size, colourway and condition
  • Short verbatim snippets of receipt text, retained alongside each extracted figure so every number can be traced back to where it was read

The stored image is the original. It may contain more than the list above — a delivery address, your name, a loyalty number — depending on what the merchant printed. Colourway does not extract those fields, but it does keep the image.

Information Colourway derives

Inventory items, landed cost, holding periods, sale prices, fees, payouts, realized profit and loss, and an append-only audit trail of every correction you make.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records IP address, timestamp, and device user-agent for authentication requests and API calls, for security and abuse prevention.
  • Operational logs. When a receipt is extracted, we log the number of pages, processing time, and cost. The contents of the receipt are not logged.

What Colourway does not collect

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs. No Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no Mixpanel, no Sentry, nothing of the kind — the app's only third-party dependency is our hosting provider's client library.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency permission is requested.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos library scanning, or device identifiers for advertising.
  • No full payment card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details. Colourway does not take payments and has no payment form.

Where your data is stored

Your account, ledger, and receipt images are stored in Canada, in the ca-central-1 region. Receipt images are held in a private storage bucket that is not publicly readable; the app retrieves them using short-lived signed links. Database access is restricted per-user at the database level, so one account cannot read another's rows.

Some processing takes place in the United States — receipt reading by Anthropic, and QuickBooks sync by Intuit if you have connected it. If you are in the EEA or UK, this means your information is transferred outside your region.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Colourway does not expire, archive, or auto-delete your ledger — a cost basis is only useful if it goes back as far as your inventory does.

When you delete your account, the deletion from our live database is immediate and permanent. Our hosting provider takes an encrypted backup of the database once a day and keeps each one for 7 days, so a copy of your data can persist in those backups for up to 7 days after you delete it, and is then gone. Backups are not readable by the app and are used only to restore the service after a failure.

Technical and operational logs are retained for 7 days.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account, inside the app. It asks you to type DELETE to confirm, and then removes:

  • Your account and login
  • Every receipt, including the stored images
  • Every transaction, tax line and line item
  • Your inventory, sales and realized profit and loss
  • Photos of the items you kept, and your profile picture
  • The edit history behind every correction
  • Your categories and their QuickBooks mapping
  • Your QuickBooks connection, which we also revoke at Intuit

Revoking at Intuit needs Intuit to answer. If they don't, the deletion still goes ahead and the app tells you to remove Colourway under Apps in QuickBooks yourself — an outage on their side will never trap you in an account you want gone.

There is no grace period and no way to undo it. Export your data first if you want to keep it — Profile → Export writes your whole ledger to a ZIP of CSVs, with the receipt images if you ask for them. Once deleted it cannot be recovered by you or by us: the daily backups described above are whole-database snapshots used to restore the service after a failure, and there is no mechanism to lift one account's data back out of them.

You can also email privacy@colourwayapp.com to request deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Most of this is available directly in the app: your ledger is visible and editable throughout, and account deletion is one screen away.

To exercise any right that is not available in the app, email privacy@colourwayapp.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

  • Canada (PIPEDA). You may access your personal information and challenge its accuracy, and may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
  • EEA / UK (GDPR). Our lawful basis is performance of a contract — providing the service you signed up for — and legitimate interests in securing it. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA). Colourway does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and does not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You will never be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to your rows is enforced at the database level rather than only in the app. Receipt images sit in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed links. QuickBooks tokens are held where the client application cannot read them at all.

No system is perfectly secure, and Colourway does not claim otherwise. If you believe your account has been compromised, email privacy@colourwayapp.com.

Children

Colourway is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, email privacy@colourwayapp.com and we will delete it.

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use Colourway after that means you accept the updated policy.
