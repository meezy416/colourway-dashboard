What Colourway is

Colourway turns photographs of receipts into a per-item cost basis for resale inventory: what you paid, what it cost to land, what it sold for, and what was left. It can post those records to QuickBooks Online if you connect it, and can produce summaries organised for a tax return.

What it is not. Colourway is a record-keeping tool, not a professional service. It does not provide tax, accounting, legal, or financial advice, and using it does not create a professional relationship of any kind. Its outputs — including the tax pack, the profit-and-loss report, and anything posted to QuickBooks — are your records, prepared from data you supplied. Before you file a return, claim a deduction, or rely on a number in any other way, review it yourself and take professional advice if you need it. You remain responsible for the accuracy and completeness of your own books.

Automatic extraction is imperfect, by nature. Colourway reads receipts using an automated system. It misreads things. Totals, dates, tax amounts, currencies and item details can all come out wrong, and a receipt that is creased, cropped, faded, in an unfamiliar format, or in a language the system handles poorly makes that more likely. The app shows you what it read and where it read it from precisely so you can check it, and it flags what it is unsure about. That checking is your responsibility, not something the app has done for you. We do not warrant that any extracted figure is correct.
