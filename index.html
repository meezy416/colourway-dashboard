Colourway
For iPhone

Receipts in,
cost basis out.

Colourway turns a photograph of a receipt into a per-item cost basis for resale inventory — what you paid, what it cost to land, what it sold for, and what was actually left.

Built for people who buy and resell sneakers and streetwear, and who are tired of a spreadsheet that never quite balances.

Watch it read a receipt

A real recording, not a mockup — the same reader that runs on your receipts, on a receipt it had never seen.

Watch what it does when it is not certain. It infers the currency from the province and the tax line, and says so. It marks shipping and duties as unsure rather than guessing a zero. Nothing is saved until you have looked at it.

The only edit is speed: reading takes about fifteen seconds and is played faster here.

How it works

Photograph the receipt

Paper, email, a screenshot of an order page. Colourway reads the merchant, date, order number, currency, subtotal, tax, shipping, duties and every line item.

Check what it read

Every figure shows the snippet of receipt text it came from, so you can see why it was read that way. Anything uncertain waits in Needs Review instead of entering your books.

Sell, and find out what you made

Record the sale with its fees. Realized profit per pair and per month, with the shipping and duties already spread across the pairs that incurred them.

What it keeps track of

Landed cost, not sticker price

Shipping, duties, brokerage and authentication spread across the pairs they belong to, so each item carries what it actually cost to get into your hands.

Bought abroad

Foreign receipts keep their own amounts and convert at the rate published on the day you bought — from the Bank of Canada or the ECB, a rate a filing can cite.

What actually sold

Marketplace, payment and shipping fees against each sale, and the effective rate each platform really takes rather than the one it advertises.

What's still on the shelf

Stock at landed cost, how long each pair has been sitting, and what is aging past the point where it was worth holding.

Books that match

Post purchases and sales to QuickBooks Online with sales tax handled properly and no duplicate entries — or export the whole ledger to CSV whenever you like.

A year, shaped for a return

The tax pack groups a calendar year into the lines a T2125 or Schedule C actually asks for, with the receipts behind them.

Two things worth saying plainly

Automatic reading is sometimes wrong. Colourway reads receipts with an automated system, and creased, cropped or unusually formatted ones are harder than clean ones. Every figure is editable, and the app shows its work so you can check it. It is a bookkeeping tool, not tax or accounting advice — the numbers are yours to review before you file on them.

Your data stays yours. Everything is stored in Canada. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of any kind. Your ledger exports at any time, and deleting your account deletes it — immediately, from inside the app.
