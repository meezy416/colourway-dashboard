From shelf to filing

Four steps, and the last one is the point.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Photograph the receipt, or import one you already have. Paper, email, screenshot.

  2. 02

    Read

    Merchant, date, line items, sizes, tax and payment method — with anything uncertain marked rather than invented.

  3. 03

    Hold the basis

    Each pair carries its landed cost, its status, and how long it has sat. Sell it and the realized figure follows automatically.

  4. 04

    File

    A calendar year of the ledger, shaped for T2125 or Schedule C, exported as a pack your accountant can open.

Privacy

Zero analytics SDKs. Not one.

There is no tracker in this app measuring how you use it. Receipt images go to one processor to be read and are not used to train anything. Your whole ledger exports to a ZIP of CSVs whenever you want it, images included, and deleting your account removes it.

The full detail is in the privacy policy, which is written to match what the app actually does rather than to cover every possibility.

  • No analytics SDK
  • No ad identifiers
  • No selling of your data
  • No training on your receipts
  • Export everything, any time
  • Delete the account and it is gone
